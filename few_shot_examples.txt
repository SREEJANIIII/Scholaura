EXAMPLE 1: RESEARCH PAPER ANALYSIS

Input:
Analyse a research paper about Machine Learning in Healthcare.

Expected Output:

Objective:
Identify how machine learning improves healthcare
prediction and diagnosis.

Methodology:
Explain the algorithms, datasets and evaluation
techniques used in the study.

Findings:
Summarize the major research findings.

Limitations:
Identify dataset limitations, bias and generalizability issues.

Research Gaps:
Suggest unexplored areas for future research.


EXAMPLE 2: MULTI-PAPER COMPARISON

Input:
Compare two research papers on Large Language Models.

Expected Output:

Paper 1:
Summarize its objective, methodology and findings.

Paper 2:
Summarize its objective, methodology and findings.

Comparison:
Identify similarities and differences in approaches,
datasets, evaluation methods and conclusions.

Research Gap:
Identify areas that require further investigation.


EXAMPLE 3: RESEARCH QUESTION ANSWERING

Input:
What methodology does this paper use?

Expected Output:

Methodology:
Explain the research approach and techniques used.

Evidence:
Provide supporting information from the document.

Source:
Mention the paper title and verified page reference.

If the information is unavailable:
State that the uploaded document does not provide
sufficient evidence to answer the question.
